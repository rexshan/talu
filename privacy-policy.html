Talu Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This page mirrors the in-app privacy policy for Talu and is intended for App Store submission and public hosting.

Last updated: March 20, 2026
On-device first Core gameplay data is stored locally on your device.
No account required The current app does not require registration or sign-in.
No tracking The current app does not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

1. Overview

Talu is designed to work primarily on your device. Based on the current version of the app, gameplay data is stored locally on the device and is not sent to a developer-operated server.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store a nickname you choose, your game difficulty preference, and your local game history and scores. This information is used only to make the app function as expected for you on your device.

3. Information We Do Not Collect Remotely

The app does not currently require account registration, does not provide online matchmaking, does not upload gameplay history to a remote server, and does not use your nickname for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

4. Tracking, Advertising, and Third Parties

The app does not currently use third-party advertising SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell or share your personal information with data brokers or advertising partners.

5. Permissions

The app is not intended to request access to sensitive personal resources such as your contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or precise location in order to provide its core gameplay features.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because app data is stored locally, you can remove most stored information directly in the app. For example, you can clear game records and reset preferences in Settings, and you can remove the locally stored nickname by logging out. Deleting the app from your device will also remove app data stored locally by the app in the normal course of iOS device management.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is a casual game and is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children through remote transmission. If future versions add online features, this policy should be updated before release.

8. Security

We aim to keep the app simple and privacy-conscious by minimizing data use and keeping gameplay information on-device where practical. However, no device or software environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. If a future version adds online services, analytics, cloud sync, sign-in providers, or advertising, this policy should be revised before those features are made available.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please email service@taluim.cc. We usually reply within 1-3 business days.
