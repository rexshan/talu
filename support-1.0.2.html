This page provides the official support contact for Azizi. For support or privacy questions, email us directly.

Contact

Email service@taluim.cc for support, privacy, or account-related questions.

service@taluim.cc

What to include

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots or screen recordings are helpful when available.

Response time

We usually reply within 1-3 business days.

Privacy

If your question is about how Azizi handles information, please review the Privacy Policy page on this site.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from the in-app account settings. Account deletion starts a 30-day recovery period. During that period, you may sign in again and explicitly choose to restore the account. If you need help accessing this option, email us and include your Tal ID when available.
